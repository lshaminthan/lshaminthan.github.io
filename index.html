Research · NLP · NIT Rourkela · 2025

Semantic Role Labelling for Indic Languages

B.Tech research project under Prof. Tapas Kumar Mishra. Semantic Role Labelling teaches a model to understand "who did what to whom" in a sentence. It works well for English, but struggles with Indian languages, which affects tools like translation, search and question answering for millions of speakers.

  • Analysed why English-focused models fail on Indic languages: flexible word order, rich case marking, agglutinative words in languages like Tamil and Hindi, and very little labelled training data.
  • Reviewed approaches from feature-based systems to LSTM, Bi-LSTM and Transformer models, and compared their strengths and weaknesses for Indic text.
  • Proposed a framework that fine-tunes multilingual XLM-RoBERTa as a BIO sequence tagger, using cross-lingual transfer from English PropBank, with LSTM and Bi-LSTM baselines.
  • Proposed a morphology-aware evaluation metric to sit alongside precision, recall and F1.
  • NLP
  • Transformers
  • XLM-R
  • Bi-LSTM
  • PyTorch
  • Cross-lingual Learning
